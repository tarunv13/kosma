Compatibility

Guna milan is eight factors taken from the Moon and nothing else — it ignores both ascendants, the 7th house, Venus, Mars, and the dashas in force. So the score is reported, but it does not lead. What leads is the set of findings that two independent kinds of factor agree on, across both charts. Where they do not agree, the topic is withheld.

Before you read the number

Two of the eight kootas carry social weight worth naming. Varna here is a classification by the Moon sign's element — a temperament class assigned by the sky, not anyone's birth caste, and it means nothing about social standing. Nadi and Bhakoot doshas are routinely used to refuse matches, usually without the classical cancellation conditions that go with them. Those conditions are checked here and reported alongside.

A dosha is a flag to examine. It is not a disqualification of a person.
